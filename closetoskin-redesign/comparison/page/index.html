What changed in version 3, and why

Built from what the category leaders do

  1. An editorial heroHer own high-resolution photo fades into its studio wall, with a short promise: “The layer you forget you’re wearing”. Large brands (Icebreaker, Janus, Hanro) all lead with one strong image and one benefit.
  2. Fibre firstEvery product card shows the real composition, like “85% wool · 15% silk”, and the menu lets you shop by fibre, the way Hanro does.
  3. Find your layerTwo questions (do you get cold easily, how much coverage) pick three products. Woolpower’s base-layer guide uses the same first question.
  4. A warmth scaleLight to warmest, worked out from wool content, as Devold and Janus grade their fabrics. Shown on every underwear product.
  5. A care label on product pagesComposition, warmth and care symbols set like the woven label inside the garment.
  6. Complete the layerEach underwear product suggests a cardigan, leggings or briefs and a piece of jewellery to go with it.
  7. Bestsellers and Gifts in the menuBestsellers first, as on Hanro and SKIMS, and a Gifts page sorted by budget for the Christmas season.
  8. Her own type and colourBodoni Moda for the brand voice, Hanken Grotesk for reading, a mono “label” face for fibre facts, and colours taken from her photography.
