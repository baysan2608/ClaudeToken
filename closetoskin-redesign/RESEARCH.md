1. BRANDS AND SCALE
- SKIMS: valued at $5bn after raising $225m (Nov 2025). Net sales expected to exceed $1bn in 2025. 18 owned US stores (https://am.gs.com/en-no/institutions/news/press-release/2025/skims).
- Hanro: no public figure for Hanro alone. Parent Huber Holding had €131.5m sales in 2015 (https://www.tt.com/artikel/11675574/huber-holding-2015-mit-23-prozent-umsatzplus-wieder-im-aufschwung). Sold in "almost 50 countries" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanro).
- Devold: revenue NOK 389.1m in 2025 (https://www.proff.no/selskap/devold-of-norway-as/langev%C3%A5g/tekstiler-og-tilbeh%C3%B8r/IGA868U07R0). Fenix Outdoor paid €35m for 65% (https://www.inderes.se/en/releases/fenix-outdoor-becomes-majority-owner-in-devold-to-build-a-leading-global-wool-brand).

- Woolpower: about SEK 300m turnover, 260 staff (EFN, Sep 2026, https://efn.se/tysk-forsvarsorder-lyfter-woolpower-i-ostersund).
- Cuyana: $30m round in 2019 (https://hig.com/news/h-i-g-growth-partners-leads-30-million-growth-financing-for-cuyana/). 5 stores listed (https://cuyana.com/pages/visit-us). No public revenue figure.

3. PATTERNS TO ADOPT (ranked by likely impact)
1. Let shoppers browse by fibre (wool-silk 70/30, 85/15, pure silk, cotton-wool-silk) as well as by garment (Hanro, SKIMS).
2. Name products the same way every time, line + garment, e.g. "Wool-Silk 70/30 Turtleneck".
3. Show a warmth scale (Light / Warm / Extra warm) on product cards and product pages (Devold, Janus).
4. Add a 3-question "Find your layer" guide: get cold easily? worn where? worn under what? (Woolpower)
5. Put fit help next to the size picker: model's height and size, a fit note for each style, a size table in cm (SKIMS, Woolpower).
6. Put a trust row under Add to cart: free delivery in Norway, delivery days, returns, Vipps (Janus).
7. Show star ratings, plus press or test quotes (SKIMS, Cuyana, Janus).
8. Explain each fibre blend in one line ("warm without bulk"), then give care instructions (Hanro).
9. Tell the origin story on the product page: Oslo since 2009, who knits it, Calderara jewellery handmade in Venice (Woolpower, Cuyana).
10. Suggest pieces that complete the layer: camisole + leggings + cardigan, with jewellery as an add-on (Woolpower).
11. Keep the hero to one seasonal line, one benefit and one button (Janus, Woolpower).
12. Add a Bestsellers link to the menu (Hanro, SKIMS).
13. Offer a waitlist for each sold-out size (SKIMS).
14. Add Christmas gifting tools: gift wrap, a gift guide, "send a hint" (Cuyana).

4. WHAT WOULD FEEL WRONG FOR CLOSETOSKIN
- Drop hype: dated launch previews, "Text SKIMS to 68805 to never miss a drop", celebrity collaborations.
- Huge menus (Devold lists 28 product families); keep it flat.
- Outdoor-technical framing (ski touring, micron counts). Use occasion and temperature words.
- Discount-heavy design: "Outlet Up to 40% off" in every menu (Devold), free returns tied to joining a loyalty programme ("Join SKIMS Rewards for Free Returns").
- Modular bag "systems" and monogram builders (Cuyana).
