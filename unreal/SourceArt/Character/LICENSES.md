Only DATA files of MakeHuman are used (no MakeHuman program code, which is AGPL). The base mesh is reshaped by our own
target recipe, refitted onto our rig, cut, re-UV-packed, dressed in original garments and textured procedurally; no
MakeHuman textures, clothes, hair or proxies are used. No other third-party asset is used.
